From the Original Pages of History

QDL · Delhi Arabic 1897

Al-Khwārizmī · a later commentary

Sharḥ mukhtaṣar al-Khwārizmī fī al-jabr wa-al-muqābalah

A 1627 Arabic manuscript copy of a commentary on al-Khwārizmī’s algebra, completed by al-Khuzāʿī in 1211. This is a later commentary, not al-Khwārizmī’s own surviving manuscript.

Delhi Arabic 1897 · British Library, Oriental Manuscripts · ff. 1r–124v · Arabic
