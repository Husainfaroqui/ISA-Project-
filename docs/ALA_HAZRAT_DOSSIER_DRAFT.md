## Contributions (draft classification)

- **Critique of the 1919 planetary-alignment prediction (Mu'in-i-Mubin)**: commentary_or_synthesis, attribution confidence medium, under_review. A treatise answering a published prediction of a planetary alignment, recalculating planetary positions on a geocentric model and arguing from gravitation against the predicted effects (as described in S1).
- **Arguments against the Earth's motion (Fauz-i-Mubin)**: theoretical_contribution, attribution confidence medium, under_review. A book presenting 105 arguments, according to the author's preface as quoted in S1, against the motion of the Earth, drawing on mathematics, philosophy, logic and the physics of his day. It is a historical argument. Modern astronomy establishes that the Earth rotates and orbits the Sun, so its conclusion is not part of modern astronomy.
