## Editorial rules

- The local-first source of truth is `content/manuscripts.json`; public pages do not depend on Supabase.
- Add only a scan that can be traced to the holding institution’s catalogue or digital collection. Keep the direct item/manifest URL, image attribution, rights statement, shelfmark, date and connection to the scholar’s actual work.
- Classify autograph, historical copy, later commentary, translation, and printed edition separately. Never imply that a copy was written by the scholar unless the catalogue establishes it.
- Do not use generated or illustrative art as a substitute. Empty states are intentional until evidence is verified.
- IIIF manifests are linked for source-side inspection. Previous/next page controls are not shown unless an actual page sequence is integrated into the record.
